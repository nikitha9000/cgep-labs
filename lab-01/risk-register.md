2\. Risk Assessment Methodology



Risk Score = Likelihood x Impact.

Likelihood Scale

Score	Description

1	Rare

2	Unlikely

3	Possible

4	Likely

5	Almost Certain

Impact Scale

Score	Description

1	Insignificant

2	Minor

3	Moderate

4	Major

5	Severe

Risk Rating

Score	Rating

1-4	Low

5-9	Medium

10-16	High

17-25	Critical

3\. Risk Treatment and Monitoring



&#x20;   Review each risk and its mitigation with the proposed risk owner.



&#x20;   Track remediation activities in the remediation plan.



&#x20;   Record evidence of implemented security controls.



&#x20;   Reassess likelihood and impact after mitigation.



&#x20;   Document residual risk and obtain risk acceptance from the authorized owner where applicable.



&#x20;   Review risks whenever infrastructure, access permissions, or project scope changes.
